Port Orientation

Your First Look at Bonaire

Bonaire is one of the Caribbean's most distinctive cruise destinations — a reef-fringed Dutch Caribbean island where marine conservation, eco-luxury and unhurried island life define every port day. With a compact capital, world-renowned snorkeling and a huge range of shore excursions, a day here can be as relaxed or as adventurous as you like.

This guide covers everything cruise passengers need, from the moment your ship arrives to when you're back on board. Ready to plan your perfect port day? Start with our Best Bonaire Shore Excursions guide.

Quick Answers for Cruise Passengers

🚢

Where will my ship dock?

Most cruise ships dock at the Kralendijk cruise pier in the heart of town. Smaller ships occasionally anchor and tender passengers ashore near the waterfront.

⏰

How much time will I have?

Most itineraries give you 7 to 10 hours in port: usually 8am or 9am until 5pm or 6pm. Always confirm your ship's all aboard time and build in a 30-minute buffer.

💵

What currency is used?

The US dollar. No currency exchange needed. Credit cards are widely accepted in Kralendijk, and ATMs are available near the pier.

🚕

How do I get around?

Taxis and rental cars are available. Most cruise passengers book organised shore excursions with included transport — the simplest option for marine park and island tours.

📋

Do I need a passport?

A valid passport is required for most visitors to Bonaire. Check your cruise line's documentation requirements before departure.

🤿

Is the snorkeling really that good?

Yes — Bonaire National Marine Park protects one of the Caribbean's healthiest reef systems. Klein Bonaire and the leeward coast offer world-class shore and boat snorkeling.

Port Terminals

Where Do Cruise Ships Dock?

Bonaire's cruise pier sits in the heart of Kralendijk, making it one of the Caribbean's most convenient port arrivals.

🚢

Kralendijk Cruise Pier

Primary Terminal

The main cruise pier is located along Kralendijk's waterfront, placing you within walking distance of shops, restaurants, dive operators and excursion pickup points. The colourful Dutch Caribbean capital unfolds directly from the gangway.

  • ✓Walking distance to Kralendijk shops and waterfront cafés
  • ✓Taxi stand and excursion departures at the pier
  • ✓Boat operators for Klein Bonaire depart nearby
  • ✓Easy access to marine park shore sites along the coast
🚤

Tendering Into Port

Occasional

On busy days or for smaller vessels, ships may anchor in the harbour and ferry passengers ashore via tender boats. Tender landings typically arrive near the Kralendijk waterfront — still conveniently close to town and excursion pickups.

  • ✓Allow extra time for tender queues in the morning
  • ✓Book excursions through operators familiar with tender timing
  • ✓Return early — tender lines build before all aboard
