Discover Bonaire Beyond
the Reef

Bonaire is far more than a diving destination. Its arid interior holds surreal pink salt flats patrolled by Caribbean flamingos, cactus-studded landscapes that feel almost desert-like, and a colourful capital where Dutch Caribbean charm meets relaxed island luxury.

The Bonaire island tour is a curated sightseeing shore excursion designed for cruise guests who want to experience the island's full character — from the southern salt works and flamingo sanctuaries to Kralendijk's waterfront and dramatic coastal viewpoints.

Every tour is built around your ship's schedule, with pier-side pickup and a comfortable return buffer before all aboard.

Tour Highlights

What You'll Experience

Flamingo Sanctuary

Visit the southern wetlands where Caribbean flamingos wade through shallow lagoons — one of Bonaire's most iconic and photogenic sights.

Salt Flats & Salt Works

Explore the surreal pink salt pans and historic Cargill salt works — a landscape of extraordinary colour and Caribbean industrial heritage.

Kralendijk Waterfront

Stroll the colourful capital — artisan shops, local galleries, waterfront cafés and the relaxed charm of Dutch Caribbean island life.

Coastal Viewpoints

Stop at dramatic lookout points along the island's rugged eastern and western shores — turquoise water against arid cactus landscapes.

Cactus & Desert Scenery

Discover Bonaire's unique arid ecology — towering cacti, iguana habitats and landscapes unlike anywhere else in the Caribbean.

Marine Park Overlooks

View the protected reef coastline from scenic vantage points — a reminder of why Bonaire is the Caribbean's marine conservation capital.
