Why Klein Bonaire Is the
Ultimate Bonaire Snorkeling Excursion

Just offshore from Kralendijk lies Klein Bonaire — an uninhabited island surrounded by some of the Caribbean's most pristine reef. Protected within Bonaire National Marine Park, its leeward shores offer crystal turquoise water, thriving coral gardens and abundant marine life just metres from the beach.

Klein Bonaire snorkeling tours are designed for luxury cruise guests who want more than a routine port day. From pier-side boat pickup, every detail — transfer timing, guided snorkeling, equipment and return to ship — is handled with the precision discerning travellers expect.

Whether this is your first Caribbean snorkel or your return to a reef you never forgot, Klein Bonaire transforms a cruise stop into something genuinely extraordinary.
