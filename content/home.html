Why Cruise Passengers
Choose Bonaire

Bonaire is one of the Caribbean's most extraordinary marine destinations — a protected reef-fringed island where turquoise water meets world-class snorkeling, and unhurried eco-luxury defines every shore day. From the uninhabited sands of Klein Bonaire to pink salt flats dotted with flamingos, it is a port that rewards travellers who seek something genuinely special.

Whether you crave pristine reef snorkeling, a catamaran sail along the leeward coast, a marine park dive experience or a relaxed island tour through flamingo sanctuaries and salt pan landscapes, Bonaire delivers premium Caribbean moments without rushing your return to ship.

Most excursions are built around cruise schedules, so you can enjoy a full, relaxed day ashore and still be back at Kralendijk pier with time to spare.

Cruise FAQ

Bonaire Shore Excursion Questions

How much time do I need in Bonaire port? ›

Most cruise ships are in port 7 to 10 hours. Klein Bonaire snorkeling (3 to 4 hours) and island sightseeing tours (3 to 4 hours) both work well if you confirm return times with your operator.

Can I snorkel at Klein Bonaire on a Bonaire cruise day? ›

Yes — Klein Bonaire is one of the most popular shore excursions from Bonaire cruise port. Most tours include boat transfer and allow 1.5 to 2 hours in the water. Book early on busy port days.

Are independent shore excursions safe for cruise passengers? ›

Licensed Bonaire operators run thousands of cruise tours each season. Book operators that guarantee ship return timing and read recent reviews. Verify prices and policies before you pay.

What currency is used in Bonaire? ›

Bonaire uses the US dollar. Credit cards are widely accepted in Kralendijk; small vendors may prefer cash for tips or beach rentals.
