Why Trafalgar Falls Is the
Ultimate Dominica Waterfall Excursion

Deep in Dominica's emerald interior, Trafalgar Falls reveals the island at its most dramatic — twin cascades known as Mother and Father Falls plunging through ancient rainforest just minutes from Roseau. Mist rises from volcanic rock, tropical ferns frame every view and the sound of rushing water fills the gorge.

Trafalgar Falls excursions are crafted for luxury cruise guests who crave authentic wilderness without sacrificing comfort. From pier-side pickup through guided rainforest trails, natural pool swims and a timely return to ship, every detail reflects the refined pace of a premium shore day.

Whether you seek an invigorating hike or a serene moment beneath cascading water, Trafalgar Falls transforms a cruise stop into one of the Caribbean's most memorable eco-adventures.
