Port Orientation

Your First Look at Dominica

Dominica is the Caribbean's Nature Island — a lush volcanic gem where rainforest waterfalls, jungle gorges, whale-filled waters and geothermal hot springs define every port day. With Roseau as a compact capital and world-class eco-adventures within easy reach, a cruise stop here rewards travellers who want wild scenery over beach-resort routine.

This guide covers everything cruise passengers need, from the moment your ship arrives to when you're back on board. Ready to plan your perfect port day? Start with our Best Dominica Shore Excursions guide.

Quick Answers for Cruise Passengers

🚢

Where will my ship dock?

Most cruise ships dock at the Roseau Cruise Berth in the capital. Smaller ships or busy days may anchor in the harbour and tender passengers ashore near the waterfront.

⏰

How much time will I have?

Most itineraries give you 7 to 10 hours in port: usually 8am or 9am until 5pm or 6pm. Always confirm your ship's all aboard time and build in a 30-minute buffer.

💵

What currency is used?

The Eastern Caribbean dollar (XCD), though US dollars are widely accepted in Roseau. Credit cards work at most tour operators and larger shops; carry small bills for taxis and market stalls.

🚕

How do I get around?

Taxis and minibus routes serve Roseau and nearby sites. Most cruise passengers book organised shore excursions with included transport — the simplest option for rainforest trails, gorges and whale-watching boats.

📋

Do I need a passport?

A valid passport is required for most visitors to Dominica. Check your cruise line's documentation requirements before departure.

🌿

Is Dominica worth it for nature?

Absolutely — Dominica is one of the Caribbean's most biodiverse islands. UNESCO-listed rainforest, twin waterfalls, volcanic gorges and year-round whale watching make it a standout nature port.

Port Terminals

Where Do Cruise Ships Dock?

Dominica's cruise berth sits in the heart of Roseau, placing rainforest excursions and the capital's markets within easy reach of the gangway.

🚢

Roseau Cruise Berth

Primary Terminal

The main cruise pier is located along Roseau's waterfront, placing you within walking distance of the Old Market, local cafés, tour operators and excursion pickup points. Lush volcanic hills rise directly behind the capital — a preview of the rainforest adventures ahead.

  • ✓Walking distance to Roseau shops, markets and waterfront cafés
  • ✓Taxi stand and excursion departures at the pier
  • ✓Short drive to Trafalgar Falls and Titou Gorge trailheads
  • ✓Whale-watching boats depart from nearby Roseau harbour
🚤

Tendering Into Port

Occasional

When the berth is full or for smaller vessels, ships may anchor in Roseau Bay and ferry passengers ashore via tender boats. Tender landings typically arrive near the waterfront — still conveniently close to town and excursion pickups.

  • ✓Allow extra time for tender queues in the morning
  • ✓Book excursions through operators familiar with tender timing
  • ✓Return early — tender lines build before all aboard
